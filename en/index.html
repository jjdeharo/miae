A guide to getting started

MIAE helps students and teachers describe generative AI use, agree how it may be used in a task and explain the process they followed.

  1. Define the task

    Identify who performs it and which product or component you are classifying: a report, its illustration or the analysis of a text.

  2. Consider the whole process

    Describe what AI contributed and what the person did with it. The first prompt, the amount of text and the number of messages do not determine the level.

  3. Explain the classification

    Justify the level through the division of work. If phases or components differ, declare their respective levels rather than automatically choosing the highest.

Levels describe how work is shared. A higher level does not mean better quality or more learning, and there is no need to go through the earlier levels.

One ecosystem report, six processes

In every case the product is a report. What changes is how it is developed. Select a level to read its full definition.

0 The person creates The person observes the ecosystem, consults sources and writes the report without generative AI at any stage. 1 AI reformulates The person provides fully developed observations, explanations and conclusions. AI improves their expression without adding content or changing the relationships between ideas. 2 AI plans AI suggests questions and an outline to guide the work. The person researches and independently develops all the explanations and the final wording. 3 The person builds AI writes an initial report. The person reconstructs its content: revises the ecological relationships, incorporates their own data and develops new explanations and conclusions of their own. 4 Co-creation The person and AI develop the report through dialogue. The person contributes observations, challenges an explanation and proposes another; both contributions shape the content as it is developed. 5 The person supervises The person provides the topic, data and requirements. AI develops the entire report; the person checks its accuracy and approves it without reconstructing or co-creating its content.
